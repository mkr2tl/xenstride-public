- `xcl-gpl/`: GPL-2.0-only, derived from ivall/xcl; see its LICENSE and README.

corresponding source, build instructions and all dependency notices. Do not relabel an
XCL derivative MIT. This is a redistribution inventory, not a legal opinion or an assurance
that every possible use of third-party game material is permitted.
